Blend aesthetics, user empathy, and product strategy. Discover career milestones and salary expectations across digital and product design disciplines.

High Growth $90k - $140k / yr

UI / UX Product Design

Craft user-centric applications, design systems, interactive prototypes, and lead usability research sessions.

Figma / FigJam User Research Design Systems

Key Milestones:

  • 1 Conduct qualitative user interviews, user journey maps, and empathy research.
  • 2 Build reusable atomic design token components and interactive Figma prototypes.
  • 3 Formulate rigorous UX case studies highlighting problem statements and quantifiable outcomes.
Roles: UX Researcher, Product Designer Review Portfolio →
Brand Identity $72k - $115k / yr

Brand & Visual Design

Define iconic company identities, typography standards, editorial publications, and marketing campaigns.

Illustrator Typography Color Theory

Key Milestones:

  • 1 Study typography hierarchy, grid systems, and vector geometry principles.
  • 2 Craft comprehensive brand identity books including logos, palettes, and tone guidelines.
  • 3 Execute multi-channel visual advertising, packaging, and digital assets.
Roles: Brand Strategist, Art Director Review Portfolio →

Motion & 3D Interactive

Transform static layouts into mesmerizing cinematic experiences using 3D rendering, physics simulation, and kinetic motion.

After Effects Blender / Cinema 4D Spline 3D

Key Milestones:

  • 1 Animation easing curves, timing, anticipatory motion, and secondary action.
  • 2 3D polygonal modeling, lighting rigs, PBR texturing, and rendering engines.
  • 3 Produce a world-class motion showreel featuring product reveal animations.
