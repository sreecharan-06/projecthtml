Medical & Healthcare Roadmaps

Dedicate your career to saving lives and clinical innovation. Understand qualification pipelines, residency timelines, and specialized domains.

Core Clinical $180k - $270k / yr

General Medicine & MD

Direct patient diagnosis, acute care treatment plans, preventive healthcare, and community health management.

Pathology Pharmacology Emergency Care

Key Milestones:

  • 1 Pre-medical sciences (Biology, Organic Chemistry) and competitive entrance (MCAT/NEET).
  • 2 4-5 years medical school coursework and supervised hospital clerkships.
  • 3 Residency training program, licensing board exams, and independent practice credentialing.
Roles: Physician, General Practitioner Consult Advisor →
Super Specialty $260k - $450k / yr

Surgery & Subspecialties

Advanced surgical precision in cardiology, neurology, orthopedics, oncology, and minimally invasive robotic procedures.

Surgical Tech Neuroanatomy Advanced Imaging

Key Milestones:

  • 1 Complete 5-year general surgery residency with intensive operating room hours.
  • 2 Pursue 2-3 year specialized surgical fellowship (e.g., Cardiothoracic or Pediatric).
  • 3 Subspecialty board certification and hospital attending privileges.
Roles: Surgeon, Cardiologist, Neurologist Consult Advisor →
Biotechnology $98k - $160k / yr

Clinical Research & Biotech

Lead revolutionary clinical trials, vaccine discovery, gene editing therapeutics, and pharmaceutical development.

CRISPR / Genetics Bioinformatics FDA Protocols

Key Milestones:

  • 1 Undergraduate & graduate studies in molecular biology, biochemistry, or genomics.
  • 2 Hands-on wet lab experience, grant writing, and academic research publications.
  • 3 Manage Phase I-III clinical trials in collaboration with healthcare institutes.
Roles: Principal Scientist, Clinical Director Consult Advisor →
